## The email

**Subject:** Small first purchase — fintech/BNPL charged-off, ~$350k face

Hello,

I run ClearSlate, a Texas debt buyer. I'm looking for a **small first purchase** and want to be upfront about size before taking your time.

**Three questions:**

**1. Do you transact at this size?**
I'm looking for roughly **$300–400k of face value**, around 1,200–1,500 accounts. If your minimum is materially larger, tell me now and I'll stop here — no hard feelings, and I'd appreciate a pointer to whoever does work at this size.

**2. What does this specific segment price at?**

- Fintech/BNPL-originated installment, charged off within 18 months
- **Balances $150–$400, average around $250**
- First or second placement, not tertiary
- Within SOL
- Texas-only to start (I'm bonded in TX; other states follow)

I'm asking about the **small-balance** band specifically, not a blended book price. My understanding is small balances discount because they're expensive to work per account — I'd like to know what that discount actually looks like rather than assuming.

**3. Email — and specifically, Reg F eligibility.**

Two different things I need to separate:

- What share of accounts carry an email address?
- For those, **did the originator send the consumer the pre-transfer notice described in 12 CFR §1006.6(d)(4)** — the one naming the email address, disclosing that others with access may see the messages, giving opt-out instructions and a 35-day window?

I'm asking because "the originator used this email" and "this email is safe-harbor eligible" are not the same thing, and only the second one lets me email. If sellers can evidence that notice, it's worth real money to me and I'd rather pay for paper that has it.

**Briefly, what I'm doing:** buying charged-off consumer paper and running voluntary, digital-first recovery. **No litigation, ever** — it's a structural commitment, not a current-phase one. I'm early and small, and this first purchase is deliberately sized to test a recovery approach rather than to make money.

Happy to talk by phone if faster.

## Notes for Ryan before sending

- **Send to 2–3 recipients** to triangulate. Candidates named in the old draft: Debexpert, DebtTrader, or fintech lenders' recovery desks directly. Verify current contacts yourself.
- **The TX bond claim.** The email says "I'm bonded in TX." **File the bond first (~$100, Tex. Fin. Code §392.101) or delete that line.** Do not write it before it's true.
- **Expect "our minimum is $1M."** That's a real and useful answer — it routes us to the contingency plan (`lender-inquiry.md`) rather than killing the project.
- **Watch for the tertiary upsell.** If a broker offers aged or third-placement paper at 1–2¢ because it fits the budget, decline. It buys more accounts and destroys external validity — the experiment stops being informative about paper we'd actually buy at scale.
